# Building Lucid's dream and nightmare for free

Lucid can reach roughly 80% of its look for $0, but the missing 20% is exactly the signature visuals: the sunset sea of clouds, the rainbow, the fire vortex and black sun, and the boss itself. Free packages cover the supporting layers well. **Staggart Stylized Skyboxes FREE** (sky), **Project-GrassFlow** (flower meadow), **CristianQiu Volumetric Light** (god rays and fog), **Quaternius and Kenney CC0 kits** (props, ruins, UI, characters), **Mixamo** (animation) and two free Asset Store music packs (Dream and Nightmare layers) are all verified or close to it for Unity 6 URP. No free asset was found for cloud vortices, rainbows, reflective water, boss models, 3D bullet-pattern systems or a lock-on kit with target switching, so those need small custom Shader Graph, VFX Graph or script work. All licenses found are jam-safe. The only mandatory credits are Incompetech (CC BY) and any CC-BY Sketchfab or Freesound item. Most Asset Store listings could not be fully verified because the pages are JavaScript-rendered, so every pick marked "check" needs a 30-second manual look before import. Nothing was downloaded or tested in 6000.0.62f1.

## Skies, foliage and water: one strong free pick each, with a hole where the clouds belong

**Sky.** The best free sky is Staggart's *Stylized Skyboxes | FREE*: Free, v1.0.0 (Mar 2026), made on Unity 6000.0.65, compatible with Built-in, URP and HDRP, Standard Asset Store EULA ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/vfx/stylized-skyboxes-free-302248)). The listing description was empty, so whether it contains sunset or gradient styles is unconfirmed; look at the screenshots first. Second is Poly Haven, where every HDRI, texture and model is CC0 with no credit required ([license](https://polyhaven.com/license)); pick sunset and overcast HDRIs for Dream and dramatic dark ones for Nightmare. Third is *AllSky Free - 10 Sky Skybox Set* (URP compatible on 6000.0.11f1, HDRP not, but skies lean realistic) ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/2d/textures-materials/sky/allsky-free-10-sky-skybox-set-146014)).

**Clouds, rainbow, vortex.** There is no verified free sea-of-clouds, rainbow or vortex sky. The heavy option is jiaozi158's MIT volumetric clouds, which require URP 14+ but make no explicit Unity 6 claim and are realistic rather than stylized ([repo](https://github.com/jiaozi158/UnityVolumetricCloudsURP)). The pragmatic route, which is my inference, is a scrolling-noise cloud plane or skybox layer in Shader Graph, a hue-ring rainbow arc, and a polar-UV swirl skybox for the Nightmare vortex and black sun. Panettone Games' *Flag and Rainbow Shaders* (free, URP and HDRP compatible, not Built-in, but flag-oriented) is only a fallback ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/vfx/shaders/flag-and-rainbow-shaders-224583)).

**Foliage and flowers.** Free Asset Store flower packs are weak or old. *Foliage Pack Free* reportedly does not work in Unity 6 URP, so avoid it ([reviews](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/vegetation/foliage-pack-free-66155/reviews)), and *Grass Flowers Pack Free* dates from 2019 ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/p/grass-flowers-pack-free-138810)). The winner is **Project-GrassFlow** (MIT, Unity 6000.0+, URP, compute-shader instanced grass with wind, a painter, and custom mesh override for flowers) ([repo](https://github.com/Mithzzx/Project-GrassFlow)). Second is MangoButtermilch's MIT grass instancer, with URP details unconfirmed ([listing](https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/github.com%2FMangoButtermilch%2FUnity-Grass-Instancer)). Flower meshes themselves must come from Quaternius nature packs or be hand-made.

**Water.** *Free Pack - Water Shader URP* by PolyOne is Free, URP only (Built-in and HDRP not compatible), v1.0 Jan 2026, but authored on 2022.3.3, so test it under Unity 6 ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/vfx/shaders/free-pack-water-shader-urp-352382)). It is the only verified free pick. daniel-ilett/water-urp (MIT) targets URP 7.1.8 and serves only as a Shader Graph reference ([repo](https://github.com/daniel-ilett/water-urp)). Do not buy Stylized Water 2, which reportedly is not compatible with Unity 6 Render Graph ([source](https://saliya1.gumroad.com/l/StylizedWater2)). No free reflective water was verified.

## Fog, post effects and VFX lean on MIT GitHub renderer features

**Fog and volumetric light.** The top pick is **CristianQiu/Unity-URP-Volumetric-Light** (MIT, last push 2026-10-16, Unity 2022.3 through 6000.6, Render Graph and Compatibility Mode, Volume-integrated so it blends Dream to Nightmare) ([repo](https://github.com/CristianQiu/Unity-URP-Volumetric-Light)). Known limits are poor transparent blending and a distance-limited fog. Second is Mirza Beig's **AERO Volumetric Fog and Mist** (Free, v1.8.0 Jun 2026, URP only, Standard EULA), a cheaper height/mist option ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/slug/277702)). sinnwrig/URP-Fog-Volumes (MIT) is a third option, but its Unity 6 support is not explicitly verified ([repo](https://github.com/sinnwrig/URP-Fog-Volumes)). Avoid the deprecated "Volumetric Fog - URP" (id 377846).

**World-swap and glitch.** Unity's built-in Fullscreen Shader Graph and Fullscreen Pass renderer feature need no download ([docs](https://docs.unity3d.com/Manual/urp/urp-shaders/fullscreen-master-stack-urp.html)). Add Cyanilux *URP_BlitRenderFeature* (MIT, pushed 2026-07-17, Render Graph support unverified) ([repo](https://github.com/Cyanilux/URP_BlitRenderFeature)) and saimarei *URPGlitch* (MIT, reportedly tested on 6000.0.0f1 and 6000.0.23f1) ([repo](https://github.com/saimarei/URPGlitch)). Do not use keijiro KinoGlitch (no license, not URP) or Josephy5's transition repo (no license).

**Particles and effects.** Use Unity's own *Particle Pack 3.3* (Free, URP compatible on 6000.0.0f1 and 6000.3.0f1) ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/p/particle-pack-127325)) and Jean Moreno's *Cartoon FX Remaster Free* (URP compatible; a reviewer says Unity 6 transparency issues vanish by enabling Depth Texture and Opaque Texture in the URP asset) ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/vfx/particles/cartoon-fx-remaster-free-109565), [reviews](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/vfx/particles/cartoon-fx-remaster-free-109565/reviews)). Unity's *Shader Graph Examples | VFX Sample Project* (Free, 6000.3.0f1, URP only, 231 MB) is the reference for dissolve, shockwave and shield graphs ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/essentials/tutorial-projects/shader-graph-examples-vfx-sample-project-372864)). *Shield Shader Free* (URP only, 2022.3.62) is a candidate for the shield bubble, with visual quality unverified ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/vfx/shaders/shield-shader-free-349218)). No free glowing-orb/projectile pack was found; retint the particle packs with additive materials. Fire vortex and ash are best built in Particle System or VFX Graph, which needs no download.

**Outlines and stylization.** *Free Outline* by Alexander Ameye (Free, v2.0.0, 6000.0.85, URP only) is the safe pick, though its feature set could not be read ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/vfx/shaders/free-outline-326925)). CristianQiu's MIT outline requires **Unity 6000.3+**, above your 6000.0.62f1, so it is not drop-in ([fork note](https://github.com/unitycoder/unity-urp-outline)). Kodrin/URP-PSX (MIT) gives a PSX look if wanted ([repo](https://github.com/Kodrin/URP-PSX)). Blob shadows: use the built-in URP Decal Projector.

## Props, architecture and characters are best served by Quaternius and Kenney CC0

**Props and architecture.** The Asset Store is a poor source here: only Hyron's *Free Low Poly Dungeon Pack* is confirmed URP (Built-in and URP compatible, HDRP not, Unity 6000.3.2, but contents undocumented) ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/dungeons/free-low-poly-dungeon-pack-398788)). *Low Poly Fuel Station* is Built-in only and explicitly not URP ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/low-poly-fuel-station-189903)), and *Arcade Machines Pack 02* is from the Unity 5.5 era ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/arcade-machines-pack-02-lowpoly-pack-79442)). Both need Edit > Rendering > Materials > Convert to URP. Raw model files from CC0 sources are pipeline-agnostic and only need URP Lit materials. Ranked: (1) **Quaternius Ultimate Modular Ruins** (CC0, 90 models) ([page](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularruins.html)); (2) **Kenney City Kit (Roads)** (CC0; roads, barriers, light posts, signs) ([page](https://www.kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads)); (3) **3dmodelscc0 City Environment Pack** (CC0; bench, payphone, street light, ATM, dumpster; 130 MB .rar) ([itch](https://3dmodelscc0.itch.io/city-environment-pack)); (4) **Electric Pole PSX** (CC0, utility pole with wires, a good liminal fit) ([itch](https://notsospecialgames.itch.io/electric-pole)); (5) **Kenney Graveyard Kit** (CC0, 90 files) for burned Nightmare dressing ([page](https://kenney.nl/assets/graveyard-kit)); (6) Quaternius Downtown City MegaKit (CC0, 315 models; the Unity URP project is the paid Source tier) ([page](https://quaternius.com/packs/downtowncitymegakit.html)); (7) Poly Haven models and ambientCG textures (CC0) ([models](https://polyhaven.com/models)). No verified free vending machine, crystals, or gas station exists; Sketchfab arcade cabinets have unconfirmed licenses (CC-BY would need credit) ([example](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/arcade-machine-4-players-a9147543ba264a8ab202144a94e33643)). Build the gas station canopy from Kenney/Quaternius blocks or ProBuilder.

**Characters and animation.** No free, rigged, URP-tagged faerie or boss was found on the Asset Store. The deprecated *Dream Word Fairies* is free, but needs material conversion ([reviews](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/characters/humanoids/fantasy/dream-word-fairies-51316/reviews)). Ranked: (1) **Quaternius Universal Base Characters / Fantasy Races** with the **Universal Animation Library** (120+ clips, universal humanoid rig, CC0; Standard tier is free) ([UAL](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html)); idle, dash, hover and fly clips are not explicitly listed. (2) **Mixamo** fills those gaps: royalty-free for commercial and non-commercial use, credit optional, requires a free Adobe ID, bars redistributing raw files and ML training ([Adobe FAQ repost](https://community.adobe.com/questions-696/mixamo-faq-licensing-royalties-ownership-eula-and-tos-589400)). Keep Mixamo FBX out of a public repo. (3) **Quaternius Ultimate Monsters** (50 animated models, CC0) for minions ([page](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html)). (4) The boss: build an eye, orb or mask from primitives plus a URP shader. Sketchfab eye models found are CC-BY ([example](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tentacle-eye-monster-e6279ed4b504415d92c18f6ba4994b53)). Quaternius pages show both "CC0" and "Quaternius Asset License"; credit both names and confirm per pack.

**Controller and camera.** Unity's *Starter Assets: First Person + Third Person* (Free, v2.0.2, URP on 6000.0.11f1 and 6000.3.0f1, Input System plus Cinemachine, **non-standard EULA** not read) pairs with the Cinemachine 3 "Lock-on Target" sample, which locks onto a boss inside a trigger zone ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/essentials/starter-assets-third-person-character-controller-196526), [docs](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.cinemachine@3.1/manual/samples-tutorials.html)). Paid lock-on kits exist ($9.99 and $14.99) but are not needed. For bullets, no free maintained 3D Unity 6 pattern library exists. Use `UnityEngine.Pool` (from background knowledge, unverified) plus a hand-written spawner. Danmokou (MIT code, separate asset terms) is likely too heavy ([repo](https://github.com/Bagoum/danmokou)).

## UI and audio: CC0 and OFL cover the interface, two free music packs cover the two worlds

**UI and fonts.** Kenney *UI Pack - Adventure* (128 sprites, CC0) ([page](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-pack-adventure)), *Cursor Pack* (150+ sprites, CC0) ([itch](https://kenney-assets.itch.io/cursor-pack)) and the Game Icons Expansion (CC0) ([OGA](https://opengameart.org/content/game-icons-expansion)) are the HUD base. They need Sprite import with 9-slice borders and no URP conversion. Fonts: **Cinzel** and **Uncial Antiqua** are SIL OFL, so commercial use is fine, but you must ship the license text with the font files ([OFL FAQ](https://openfontlicense.org/how-to-use-ofl-fonts/)). TextMeshPro is integrated into uGUI in Unity 6; a Font Asset Creator bug was reported in 6000.0.33f1 with fix status unverified ([forum](https://discussions.unity.com/t/textmesh-pro-in-unity-6/1580163)). No petal-themed HUD pack exists, so use 9-slice panels or Image fill.

**Audio.** Music is a pair: Dream = Berk Ace *Free Dreamy Ambient Music Pack* (v1.1, Apr 2022) ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/audio/ambient/free-dreamy-ambient-music-pack-218266)) and Nightmare = Millan Brothers *Free Dark Drones, Mystery & Sci-fi Ambients* (Sept 2023, 212.7 MB, reportedly six loopable ~2 min tracks) ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/audio/ambient/free-dark-drones-mystery-sci-fi-ambients-music-pack-233667)). Both are Standard EULA with no attribution needed; audition loops and matching length and key before crossfading. CC0 Nightmare alternates on OpenGameArt: "Abandoned passages" (seamless) ([OGA](https://opengameart.org/content/abandoned-passages-horror-ambience-loop)). Boss music: Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0, credit required in the exact format on its FAQ) ([FAQ](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html)); no specific title was verified. SFX: Kenney Sci-fi Sounds (70 files, CC0) ([page](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds)), Interface Sounds, Impact Sounds (all CC0 per [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/support)), Lentikula Sci-Fi Weapon Shots (CC0) ([itch](https://lentikula.itch.io/sci-fi-weapon-shots-sfx-freecc0)), Dustyroom *Free Casual Game SFX Pack* ([listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/audio/sound-fx/free-casual-game-sfx-pack-54116)). For wind, whooshes and glitch, use the Sonniss GDC bundles (no attribution, no redistribution of files, 23.5 GB for 2024) ([Sonniss](https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc/)) or Pixabay ([license](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/)). Freesound needs a login and credit for CC BY; avoid CC BY-NC and BY-SA items ([FAQ](https://freesound.org/help/faq/)). Dream/Nightmare filtering (low-pass on one layer, distortion on the other) can be done in the Unity AudioMixer. For voice, Kokoro TTS (Apache 2.0, per a secondary source) pre-rendered to wav avoids shipping code ([source](https://www.promptquorum.com/power-local-llm/piper-vs-kokoro-tts)).

## License, login and compatibility flags, and the shopping order

Every Asset Store item needs a Unity ID to add to My Assets (assumed from general knowledge, not shown on listings); Mixamo needs a free Adobe ID; Freesound needs an account; GitHub, Poly Haven, Kenney and Quaternius need none. Itch.io items are name-your-own-price, so $0 works. Asset Store EULA items may ship in your build but must not be committed raw to a public repo. Credit anyway in CREDITS.md.

| Flag | Items |
|---|---|
| Not URP or old, needs conversion | Low Poly Fuel Station (Built-in only); Arcade Machines Pack 02 (Unity 5.5); Dream Word Fairies (deprecated); Grass Flowers Pack Free (2019) |
| Reported broken in Unity 6 URP | Foliage Pack Free; Stylized Water 2 (paid, Render Graph) |
| URP-only (fine here) | PolyOne Water, AERO Fog, Free Outline, Shield Shader Free, Shader Graph Examples |
| Built on older Unity, test first | PolyOne Water (2022.3.3), Shield Shader (2022.3.62), Flag and Rainbow (2021.3), Cartoon FX Remaster (listing to 2021.3) |
| Needs newer Unity than 6000.0.62f1 | CristianQiu Outline (6000.3+) |
| No license or unclear, avoid | keijiro KinoGlitch, Josephy5 Transition, unconfirmed Sketchfab models, Starter Assets EULA (non-standard, read it) |
| Attribution required | Incompetech (CC BY 4.0), any CC BY Freesound/OGA/Sketchfab item, MIT GitHub items (keep copyright notice) |
| Deprecated | HMP LUTs Free, Volumetric Fog - URP (377846) |

Shopping list in priority order: (1) Starter Assets Third Person plus Cinemachine 3 Lock-on sample; (2) Staggart Stylized Skyboxes FREE and a few Poly Haven HDRIs; (3) CristianQiu Volumetric Light; (4) Project-GrassFlow; (5) Quaternius Universal Base Characters and Animation Library, then Mixamo for hover and dash; (6) Berk Ace and Millan Brothers music; (7) Kenney Sci-fi Sounds, Interface Sounds and UI Pack Adventure, plus Cinzel; (8) Unity Particle Pack 3.3 and Cartoon FX Remaster Free; (9) Quaternius Ultimate Modular Ruins, Kenney City Kit Roads and Graveyard Kit; (10) PolyOne Water, AERO Fog, URPGlitch, Cyanilux Blit; (11) the PSX pole and the 3dmodelscc0 pack, Shader Graph Examples as reference.

## Conclusion

The free ecosystem is strong on infrastructure and weak on spectacle: the things a jam entry gets remembered for (cloud floor, rainbow, vortex sky, boss, projectile look, reflective water) are all custom work, and they share one skill set (Shader Graph, VFX Graph, URP Volumes). Budget that work first and treat packs as set dressing.

Gaps needing a manual check or custom work: Staggart skybox contents; whether the PolyOne water, Shield Shader and Flag/Rainbow packs run cleanly on 6000.0.62f1 Render Graph; Hyron pack contents; Starter Assets EULA text; Quaternius per-pack license (CC0 vs QAL); UAL hover/dash clips; music loop quality; Sketchfab licenses for arcade cabinets and eye monsters; Unity 6 support of jiaozi158 clouds, URP-Fog-Volumes and Cyanilux Blit; and any asset for vending machine, crystals, gas station, sentient plant, boss music, 3D bullet patterns, reflective water, free LUT or bloom presets. Last-update dates for Quaternius and Kenney packs, and ambientCG contents, were not fetched. Also check jam rules on AI content before using the AI-generated synthwave CC0 pack ([itch](https://swarajthegreat.itch.io/retro-synthwave-music-pack)).
